Privacy Policy — Focus Realms

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Focus Realms is a focus-timer application developed by Atlas Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information we collect

Focus Realms does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information.

Your focus-session settings and statistics may be stored locally on your device so that the app can provide its features. This information is not sent to us.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app uses your device’s notification permission to send local focus reminders. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Audio and app content

Focus Realms provides ambient audio and visual focus environments. Using these features does not send personal information to Atlas Studio.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties because we do not collect it.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you reset the app’s data or uninstall the app. Because we do not receive this data, we cannot access or delete it for you.

Children’s privacy

Focus Realms is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to provide personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The latest version will always be available on this page, with an updated effective date.
